# Proposal templates (応募用テンプレート)

Fill in the `[brackets]` before sending. Rules for these templates:

- Do not claim client work, employers, results or years of experience that are not true.
  The only project referenced is the self-initiated portfolio project, and it says so.
- Quote numbers only from `output/summary.txt` and `output/verification_report.md`, and always say the data is synthetic.
- Replace the closing question with something specific from the client's job post.
- Keep the bracketed sentence `[I can work in Japanese and English.]` only if you can really communicate in English (delete it otherwise).
- The Japanese templates use headings and short lines on purpose: long single paragraphs are rarely read on CrowdWorks.

---

## 1. Self-introduction (English) — about 205 words

Hello, I'm [Your name], a freelancer who specializes in Excel data cleaning and CSV cleaning with Python.

I build small, reliable automation tools that turn messy customer lists into files you can actually use: consistent phone numbers, dates and company names, invalid e-mail addresses set aside, and duplicates identified. My rule is to change only what I can change with certainty. Anything ambiguous, such as a date like 03/04/2024, is kept as it was and listed for you to decide, together with a suggested fix.

As a self-initiated portfolio project (all data is fictional, no client data), I built an Excel / CSV Data Cleaner. On a synthetic 1,300-row sample it separates clean, invalid, duplicate and needs-review rows, records every changed cell in a change log, and never touches the original files. The repository includes the sample input and output, a verification report, and unit tests, so you can see exactly how it behaves. The verification is a consistency check on synthetic data, not a promise about your data, so I always start with a small paid or trial sample of your file.

[I can work in Japanese and English.] I deliver Excel files with a short summary of what was changed and why.
